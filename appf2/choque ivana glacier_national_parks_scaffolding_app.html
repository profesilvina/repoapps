Grammar Rules: Comparatives & Superlatives

💡 Quick Reminder: Use Comparatives to compare 2 things (e.g., A is colder than B). Use Superlatives to pick the #1 in a whole group (e.g., A is the coldest in the country).

Adjective Type Comparative (2 things) Superlative (The #1)
Short (1 syllable)
cold, old, large		 Adjective + -er + than
colder than		 the + Adjective + -est
the coldest
Long (2+ syllables)
famous, humid		 more + Adjective + than
more famous than		 the most + Adjective
the most famous
Irregulars
good, bad		 better than the best

Common Mistakes to Avoid!

  • Never double up: Do not say "more colder" or "the most oldest".
  • Check for key words: Use than for comparatives and the for superlatives.
